## OUR STORY

RvFox was born on a dealer lot.

Founder David was standing next to a gleaming Class A diesel, clipboard in hand, trying to fact-check the salesman’s claims on his phone. The specs didn’t add up. The “no recalls” story turned out to be wrong. The price felt high but there was nothing solid to compare it to.

He bought the RV anyway — and immediately wished he hadn’t.

That frustration became the mission: “What if buyers could walk onto any lot with the same information the dealers have?”

We built the Carfax for Motorhomes — verified manufacturer specs, live NHTSA recalls, real market framing, finance tools, and AI research in one phone-ready app.

Buyer-first. Always. **Verified & True** is the promise.

## HOW TO USE THE APP

| Tab | What to teach |
|-----|----------------|
| **RvFacts** | Year → Make → Model → Floorplan (optional) → report. VIN + recalls. Heart to save. |
| **RvCal** | Price / financed / trade · ZIP tax · credit band → payment scenarios (educational). |
| **RvTow** | Truck year–make–model–trim vs coach tow limits. |
| **RvGrok** | Ask anything RV — specs, ownership, accessories. Estimates → verify before buy. |
| **RvTrips** | Destination + RV profile for RV-aware planning. |
| **More** | Story, Help, Feedback, Privacy, Pro access, Premium. |

First-time script: “Start on RvFacts, open a report, use RvCal for payments, RvTow for the truck, ask me here, save with the heart, story under More.”
