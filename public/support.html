Help & Support

No account is required. Core catalog and calculators work without signing in.

What’s in the app

  • RvFACTS — year / make / model / floorplan specs, market bands, NHTSA recalls
  • RvCal — payment estimates with ZIP tax context
  • RvTow — truck/SUV match
  • RvTrips — routing, campgrounds, free dump stations
  • RvGrok — AI chat, optional voice and camera

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases — the full suite is included. Market figures and AI answers are estimates for education — not an official appraisal or an offer of credit.

Need help

Email contact@rvfox.app with your device, OS version, which tab you were using, and what you expected.
