Privacy Policy

RvFox is an RV research app (specs, recalls, payments, towing, trips, and an AI assistant). We do not sell your personal information.

What stays on your phone

Saved RVs, chat history, voice settings, and calculator inputs are stored on your device. We do not require an account.

What we use while you use the app

  • Location (optional) — only if you tap to sort dump stations or campgrounds by distance. Not used in the background.
  • Camera / photos (optional) — VIN scan and showing a picture to the AI assistant.
  • Microphone / speech (optional) — Live Voice and talk-to-type. Not recorded to our servers as a voice archive.
  • Chat text and photos you send — processed to answer you (via our AI provider, xAI). Do not send passwords or full payment card numbers.
  • Addresses you type for routing — sent only to compute a route (OpenStreetMap / OSRM).
  • Year / make / model / VIN you look up — sent to NHTSA for official recall data.

Third parties

NHTSA.gov (U.S. government recalls). OpenStreetMap contributors (maps/routing). xAI (Grok) for chat, vision, and voice. We do not run ads or sell lists.

Kids

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

Deny location, camera, or mic in iPhone Settings and those features stay off. Clear app data / reinstall to wipe on-device history. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by CCPA. Email privacy@rvfox.app.
